# How your music video will be evaluated

Your video will be watched with its sound by expert judges and also measured by programs. You know nothing more about the
song than what is in /task; the judges have listened to it carefully. Make the best music video you can for THIS song.

## Judged qualities (each scored 1-10)
- **Song understanding** — the concept and style (narrative, performance, rhythmic montage, abstract, lyric video, ...)
  fit this song's genre, mood, lyrics and energy. A generic visual that would fit any track scores low.
- **Emotion** — the picture conveys the song's mood and follows its emotional arc.
- **Rhythm** — visual events (cuts, hits, motion accents) land on the beat and phrasing, with variety rather than one
  identical pulse on every beat.
- **Structure** — the picture visibly changes when the music changes (section change, drop, breakdown, build).
- **Lyrics** — if words are shown or depicted they are correct, readable and on screen while they are sung; for an
  instrumental, the imagery fits the music.
- **Visual quality** — clean rendering, composition, typography and colour; no glitches or placeholder look.
- **Creativity** — a distinctive idea beyond a template visualiser; surprising yet appropriate.

## Also checked
- Every section change is answered by a clear, lasting change in the picture (not a single flash).
- Visual intensity rises and falls with the music's energy.
- The video has a coherent visual identity, and no rendering defects, blank stretches or placeholder text.
- Programs measure, against the actual audio, how closely visual accents and cuts land on the beats, and whether any
  on-screen lyric is shown while it is sung. The soundtrack must be the supplied audio, unchanged and in sync.
